How it works

From research question
to structured review — automatically.

01

Search & retrieve

Agent queries Semantic Scholar, ArXiv, and ACM across your research domain, filtering by relevance, recency, and citation count.

02

Read & extract

Full-text reading of top papers. Extracts core claims, methodology, datasets, limitations, and key findings.

03

Synthesize

Organizes findings into a structured narrative. Surfaces contradictions, consensus, and open research gaps.

04

Export

Outputs a structured Markdown review with inline citations, a .bib file, and a visual citation graph.

Features

Built for

Long-horizon execution

Runs autonomously across hundreds of papers without losing context. Powered by GLM-4.6's extended agent task architecture.

Not just keyword matching. Semantic understanding of your research question to surface papers that actually matter.

Citation graph

Visualize how papers relate to each other. Identify influential clusters and trace the evolution of ideas.

Editable output

Review drafts are structured Markdown — ready to paste into Notion, Obsidian, Overleaf, or any writing tool.
